# Open Ledger IL for accounting firms

Run your clients' books in one Ledger, on your firm's own Cloudflare account. Each client is a separate business with its own numbering, signing key, files and owner.

The firm edition

## Built for many clients

You choose it in the installer with one tick. The software is the same Open Ledger IL, registered with the Israel Tax Authority.

### Any number of clients

עוסק פטור, עוסק מורשה and companies, each a separate business with its own tax id and gapless numbering.

### Walls between clients

A client owner reaches their own business only. An automated test checks every route on every release.

### One Clients page

VAT due or ceiling use, the Tax Authority login, certificate end dates, open payment requests and expenses to review.

### Each client signs as itself

A signing key per business, so every PDF carries the client’s own name.

### Your team, one grant

Admins, assistants and read-only accountants reach every client, present and future.

### A client who leaves takes everything

Export by year, a new Ledger continues the numbering, and the books stay with you for the retention period.

## Who reaches what

| Person | Reaches |
| --- | --- |
| Ledger admin | Every business as owner, and the settings of the whole Ledger |
| Firm staff, assistant | Every business: clients, documents, sending, expenses. No settings |
| Firm staff, accountant | Every business, read only, with the features you allow |
| Client owner | Their own business only |

## Firm edition and personal edition

| | Personal | Firm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Businesses | One עוסק file, plus companies | Any number of any type |
| Settings of the whole Ledger | Any owner | Ledger admins only |
| Team access | Granted per business | One grant reaches every client |
| Signing key | One key, or one per business | One per client |
| Clients page | No | Yes |
| Cloudflare plan | The free plan works | Workers Paid, $5 a month |

## What it costs

- Firm pricing is set per firm, by the number of clients. Talk to us.
- Cloudflare bills you directly: Workers Paid from $5 a month, and Cloudflare Access seats above 50 users. Each person who signs in is one seat.
- Your clients’ data stays in your firm’s Cloudflare account. MTN holds no copy.
