# Connect to the Tax Authority for allocation numbers

Sign the Ledger in to the Israel Tax Authority once, so tax invoices get their allocation numbers by themselves, and reconnect every 90 days.

Updated 2026-10-17

A tax invoice to an Israeli עוסק מורשה above the threshold needs an allocation number (מספר הקצאה) from the Tax Authority. With the connection, the Ledger asks for the number when you finalize the invoice and prints it on the document.

An עוסק פטור issues no tax invoices, so it needs no connection. Connect later, when you become עוסק מורשה.

## Connect

1. In the Ledger, open **ITA** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Connect to ITA**.
3. The Tax Authority's sign-in page opens. Sign in with your Tax Authority user: the user code and the one-time code sent to your phone. Use the business's own user, the one for its tax number.
4. Approve the request. You come back to the Ledger, and the page shows **Connected**, with the number of days left.

The connected edition goes through MTN's approved Tax Authority app, so there is no app of your own to register.

## Every 90 days

The Tax Authority login lasts 90 days. The ITA page shows **Login ends in N days**. With Slack alerts on, the Ledger warns you before. When it ends, open **ITA** and click **Connect again**. An invoice still waiting for a number shows in the **Allocation queue** on the same page. After you reconnect, click **Retry now**.

## When a number is refused

The ITA page shows the reason. The choices are to cancel the invoice, continue without a number with a notice on the document, or switch to reverse charge with the client's agreement. Ask your accountant which fits.
